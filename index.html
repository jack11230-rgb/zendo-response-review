Zendo Response: East Coast Crisis Response Proposal

A proposal to bring Zendo-style psychedelic peer support out of festivals and into East Coast neighborhoods year-round: local volunteer chapters, a responder app for crisis alerts, up to 3 trained responders per call with remote LCSW and MD oversight, an on-call resources library (Zendo SIT refreshers, guides, ask the LCSW/MD), neighborhood Safe Zones, hospital liaisons, and a shared Resource Board. The pilot chapter shown here is Brooklyn – Coney Island.

Please read: this is a volunteer-drafted concept shared for discussion. It is not an official Zendo Project or MAPS product, plan, or endorsement. All people, encounters, places, hospitals and numbers in the mockups are fictional sample data. Please don't share outside the review group.

The idea in brief

What the app and program would do for a volunteer on shift.

Crisis alerts

Chapter hotline reports reach on-duty volunteers within ~1.5 mi; up to 3 responders can join a call.

Clinical oversight

Every encounter has a remote licensed social worker in chat and a psychiatrist on call for medical questions.

On-call resources

A quick-help library during a call: Zendo SIT training refreshers, guides, and one-tap “ask the LCSW/MD”.

Escalation built in

911 / 988 one tap away, clear escalation criteria, and nearby psych ERs with chapter hospital liaisons.

Safe Zones

Volunteer-hosted quiet rooms in community spaces where a guest can come down safely.

Chapters & community

Local chapters, event shift signups, and a Resource Board for detox beds, housing, therapy and more.

What we want feedback on

Everything is open, but these are the decisions we most need input on. Reply in the group thread with the number and your thoughts.

  1. Volunteer tier names“Tier 1 Sitter” and “Tier 2 Crisis Responder / Shift Lead” are placeholders. Better names?
  2. Alert dispatchFirst volunteer to accept within 1.5 mi gets the call, or auto-pair two responders every time?
  3. Responders per callAllow up to 3 responders on one call? When should a 2nd/3rd join, and who leads?
  4. Clinical oversightLCSW vs LMSW for remote oversight, and should oversight clinicians and the on-call MD be paid roles?
  5. On-call resources libraryWhat belongs in the quick-help screen: SIT refreshers, guides, ask-the-LCSW/MD. What's missing?
  6. HospitalsSwap the fictional hospitals for real Brooklyn ERs/CPEPs? Which ones, and who reaches out to them?
  7. Safe Zone hostingWho hosts (community centers, faith spaces, volunteers' homes?), hours, and minimum standards.
  8. Privacy & Serenity syncWhat should (and shouldn't) be recorded and synced to Serenity? No names or identifiers in notes?
  9. Chapter boundariesIs “Brooklyn – Coney Island” the right size for a chapter? How should we draw them?
  10. Resource Board moderationWho moderates, how we vet tips, and how to keep guest details out of posts.
  11. BrandingThe “Zendo Response” name, palette and look. Should it feel closer to or further from Zendo Project?
